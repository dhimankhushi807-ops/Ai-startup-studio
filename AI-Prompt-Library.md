# AI Startup Studio — AI Prompt Library

## 1. Startup Idea Generation

**Purpose:** Generate new startup ideas.

**Prompt:**

> Generate 5 innovative startup ideas based on the user's selected industry, skills, target audience, and current market needs. For each idea, provide the problem, solution, target customers, unique value proposition, and revenue model.

## 2. Idea Validation

**Purpose:** Evaluate a startup idea.

**Prompt:**

> Analyze the given startup idea for feasibility, customer need, market potential, uniqueness, scalability, competition, and possible challenges. Provide a validation score and recommendations for improvement.

## 3. Brand Name Generation

**Purpose:** Create suitable brand names.

**Prompt:**

> Generate 10 creative, memorable, and relevant brand names for the given startup idea. Make the names easy to pronounce and suitable for the target audience.

## 4. Tagline Generation

**Purpose:** Create startup taglines.

**Prompt:**

> Generate 10 short and memorable taglines that communicate the startup's main value and appeal to its target audience.

## 5. Target Audience Analysis

**Purpose:** Identify potential customers.

**Prompt:**

> Analyze the startup idea and identify its primary and secondary target audiences. Describe their needs, problems, preferences, and buying behavior.

## 6. Market Research

**Purpose:** Understand the target market.

**Prompt:**

> Analyze the target market for the given startup idea. Identify market trends, customer needs, opportunities, threats, and important factors affecting business growth.

## 7. Competitor Analysis

**Purpose:** Analyze competition.

**Prompt:**

> Analyze the major competitors related to the given startup idea. Compare their strengths, weaknesses, products, target customers, and opportunities for differentiation.

## 8. Business Model Generation

**Purpose:** Create a business model.

**Prompt:**

> Create a suitable business model for the given startup idea. Include the value proposition, customer segments, channels, key activities, resources, partners, cost structure, and revenue streams.

## 9. Marketing Strategy

**Purpose:** Develop a marketing plan.

**Prompt:**

> Create a practical marketing strategy for the startup. Recommend suitable digital marketing channels, content strategies, customer acquisition methods, promotional ideas, and key performance indicators.

## 10. Business Plan Generation

**Purpose:** Create a structured business plan.

**Prompt:**

> Create a professional business plan for the given startup idea. Include the executive summary, problem, solution, target market, competition, business model, marketing strategy, operations, risks, and growth strategy.

## 11. SWOT Analysis

**Purpose:** Analyze business strengths and risks.

**Prompt:**

> Perform a SWOT analysis for the given startup. Clearly identify its Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats, and provide practical recommendations.

## 12. Startup Improvement

**Purpose:** Improve an existing startup idea.

**Prompt:**

> Review the given startup concept and suggest improvements to its product, target audience, business model, branding, marketing strategy, and competitive positioning.

## 13. Pitch Deck Content

**Purpose:** Prepare startup pitch content.

**Prompt:**

> Create concise pitch deck content for the given startup. Include the problem, solution, market opportunity, product, business model, competition, competitive advantage, and growth strategy.

## 14. Final Startup Summary

**Purpose:** Generate a complete startup overview.

**Prompt:**

> Create a professional summary of the startup including its name, problem, solution, target audience, value proposition, business model, competitive advantage, marketing approach, and future growth opportunities.

---

## Prompt Workflow

**User Input → Idea Generation → Idea Validation → Branding → Market Research → Competitor Analysis → Business Model → Marketing Strategy → Business Plan → Pitch Preparation**

## Conclusion

This prompt library provides reusable AI prompts for the major features of AI Startup Studio. It helps users systematically transform an initial startup idea into a structured and business-ready concept.
